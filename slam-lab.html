RoomFit uses browser SLAM and now checks whether the estimated floor agrees with any reliable surface result available from the tracking engine. It is still experimental: physical scale and floor anchoring must be measured on your phone.

Start with a compact 27 cm wooden footstool rather than a large sofa. After tracking reaches NORMAL, select any furniture below and test its real-world placement. All dimensions are approximate model sizes, not certified product measurements.

Choose furniture and aim at the floor. Place once when the target is green. Amber means the location was rejected. No holding or manual resizing. Press Done to walk around and inspect.
